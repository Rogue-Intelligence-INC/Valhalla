## 💼 **BUSINESS ENTITY SETUP**

### **If Filing as Individual**
- Use personal name and address
- May qualify for small entity status (50% fee reduction)
- Simpler process but less business protection

### **If Filing as Corporation**
- **EIN Required**: Must have Employer Identification Number
- **Corporate Resolution**: Board resolution authorizing patent filing
- **Registered Agent**: May need registered agent in some states
- **Large Entity Fees**: Standard USPTO fees apply

### **Small Entity Qualification**
You may qualify for 50% fee reduction if:
- Individual inventor, OR
- Small business (<500 employees), OR  
- Nonprofit organization

**Reduced Fees**: $160 per application × 4 = $640 total
