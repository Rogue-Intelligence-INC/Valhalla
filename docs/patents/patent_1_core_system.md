# PROVISIONAL PATENT APPLICATION

### FIELD OF THE INVENTION

This invention relates to autonomous artificial intelligence systems for scientific discovery, specifically to a multi-dimensional system capable of independently discovering and validating universal physical laws through quaternary physical system architecture with unlimited computational resources.

### BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Current artificial intelligence systems for scientific discovery are limited by:
1. Predefined constraints and hard-coded thresholds
2. Limited dimensional exploration capabilities  
3. Inability to autonomously discover fundamental physical laws
4. Resource limitations that prevent unlimited exploration
5. Lack of natural physical interaction modeling

Existing systems require human intervention to define search spaces, set parameters, and interpret results. No current system can autonomously explore unlimited dimensional spaces to discover new physical laws without human guidance.

   - Unlimited complexity growth potential

### DETAILED DESCRIPTION

#### Quaternary Physical System Architecture

The system employs four specialized subsystems that collectively model all known physical phenomena:

**BlackHole System**: Processes gravitational effects, spacetime curvature, and relativistic phenomena using advanced mathematical models that simulate event horizons, gravitational lensing, and time dilation effects.

**MeshBrain System**: Handles information processing, quantum entanglement effects, and network-based phenomena through interconnected processing nodes that can model complex information flow patterns.

**MultiNova System**: Manages energy distribution, thermodynamic processes, and explosive phenomena by simulating energy cascades, phase transitions, and critical point behaviors.

**BaseForce System**: Coordinates fundamental force interactions including electromagnetic, weak nuclear, and strong nuclear forces through unified field equations.

The Voyager system automatically expands dimensional exploration based on data complexity:

1. **Unknown Signal Detection**: Uses dynamic thresholds based on average point distances rather than fixed values
2. **Dimensional Expansion**: Automatically increases dimensions when new patterns are detected
3. **Form Discovery**: Identifies and validates new physical phenomena
4. **Adaptive Navigation**: Explores higher-dimensional spaces intelligently

#### Natural Field Evolution Networks

The system employs natural cellular networks that:

1. **Calculate Natural Interactions**: Using 8-dimensional force vectors without hard-coded thresholds
2. **Enable Autonomous Fusion**: Cells with positive interactions naturally fuse based on physical attraction

4. **Grow Unlimited Complexity**: No artificial caps on system complexity or intelligence
