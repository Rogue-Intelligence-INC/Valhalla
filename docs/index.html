Abstract摘要

Deployed AI systems accumulate experience outside the computing substrate — in prompts, indices and rules — where it cannot change what is computed next. Valhalla tests the alternative: experience written into a persistent structure (Tile and Stem populations under a global algebraic criterion, Fate), with computation thereafter routed through that structure. Under a controlled ablation protocol (15 seeds), removing the learned connections returns withheld multi-hop answers to a fixed 45.5% floor, a structural causal effect of +11.6 ± 8.5 pp (positive in 13/15 seeds); clearing stored bytes changes nothing. Continual-learning forgetting on an industry-style battery is near-eliminated (backward transfer −0.7 pp), and a single deterministic programme runs 50 rounds from ingress to scored egress. Long-range (4–5 hop) composition, topology specificity, and directed-curriculum gains are not established and are reported as such. 已部署的 AI 系统把经验积累在计算基底之外——提示词、索引与规则——无法改变下一步的计算。Valhalla 检验另一种可能：经验写入持久结构（全局代数判据 Fate 之下的 Tile 与 Stem 种群），其后的计算经由该结构路由。在受控消融协议下（15 种子），移除所学连接使留出多跳题回到固定下限 45.5%，结构因果效应 +11.6 ± 8.5pp（13/15 种子为正）；清空存储字节则毫无变化。行业式电池上持续学习的遗忘基本消除（后向迁移 −0.7pp），且单一确定性程式可从入口到评分出口完整运行 50 轮。长程（4–5 跳）组合、拓扑特异性与定向课程增益未成立，照实报告。

2The system系统

Valhalla is one system with coupled responsibilities. Material enters byte-wise through Fate’s segmentation; every fragment is stored in all four quad operators (BlackHole, MeshBrain, MultiNova, BaseForce) — eight accounts per fragment, Tile-side and Stem-side — and the populations’ own dynamics (energy flow, split/merge, autonomous quad maintenance) decide what the structure becomes. Fate is the global measurement and scheduling layer: its quadratic cluster equation judges convergence, bundles parallel connection strands into single nerves, and opens long-range escape nerves toward distant strong signals. Fate does not veto body dynamics.Valhalla 是一个职责耦合的单一系统。材料逐字节经 Fate 分割入口进入；每段材料存入全部四个四元算子（BlackHole、MeshBrain、MultiNova、BaseForce）——每段八份账，Tile 侧与 Stem 侧各四——结构成为什么样，由种群自身动力学（能量流动、分裂/合并、四元自主维护）决定。Fate 是全局度量与调度层：其二元一次簇方程判断收敛、把平行连接束集束为单条神经、并向远方强信号开长程逃逸神经。Fate 不一票否决身体动力学。

Mechanisms corrected into place (2026-11-02 – 03)近期归位的机制（2026-11-02 – 03）

M1Split dynamics, restored分裂动力学，已恢复原版

Splitting follows the original field rule — energy > 1.5× population mean, at most 10 splits per cycle, energy flow rate ×0.1. A de-hardcoding change that widened this gate caused runaway growth; the defect was found by measurement and reverted to the designed rule.分裂遵循原版场规则——能量 > 1.5× 种群平均、每周期至多 10 次、能量流率 ×0.1。一次去硬编码改动曾放宽此门导致失控增长；缺陷由测量定位并已回退至设计规则。

M2Fate bundlingFate 集束

Neighbours whose distances fit under the cluster equation’s convergence rule are bundled into one nerve (target: strongest field signal in the bundle). Link count fell 19.86M strands → ≈49K nerves (−99.8%); 15-seed scoring went from >1 h to ≈1 min.距离在簇方程收敛规则下拟合的邻居集束为一条神经（目标取束内场强最高者）。连接数从 1986 万股降至约 4.9 万条神经（−99.8%）；15 种子评分从逾 1 小时降至约 1 分钟。

M3Two-layer escape双层逃逸

The quad’s designed escape law (energy > √(2·g·d), BlackHole event horizon) — dead code since import — now runs inside autonomous quad maintenance. Independently, Fate opens one long-range nerve per cell toward a distant strong signal. The layers do not interfere: storage-layer exemptions vs. graph-layer connectivity.四元原设计逃逸法则（能量 > √(2·g·d)，黑洞视界）——自导入即死代码——现已在四元自主维护中运行。独立地，Fate 为每个细胞向远方强信号开一条长程神经。两层互不干扰：存储层管豁免，图层管连通。

M4Equation adaptation, bound to Fate方程自适应，绑定 Fate

The cluster equation adapts only on material Fate itself has just judged (inside passive reception); judgement itself never modifies the equation. Live end-state after 50 rounds: 0.7155x² − 1.8912x + 0.6700, threshold 0.0100.簇方程只在 Fate 刚判定过的材料上自适应（被动接收内）；判定本身永不修改方程。50 轮后的活终局：0.7155x² − 1.8912x + 0.6700，阈值 0.0100。

5Negative results & limitations阴性结果与局限

Reported as results作为结果报告
  1. No directed-curriculum gain at two scales (C−A = +1.0 ± 10.1 pp, 15 seeds, three scoring instruments). Lesson order does not measurably matter; contact volume is what the body uses.定向课程增益未检出（两规模；C−A = +1.0 ± 10.1pp，15 种子，三种评分仪器）。课序无可测影响；身体用的是接触量。
  2. No topology specificity: cultivated bodies sit at the 7th percentile of a degree-preserving shuffle null on path measures. The network forms; that it is better than random at equal degree is not shown.拓扑特异性未成立：路径度量上培养体位于保密度重排零假设的第 7 分位。网络形成；其在等度下优于随机未证。
  3. Long-range composition deficit: 4–5 hop accuracy 28.9% under bundled nerves, below the earlier unbundled configuration. The recovery path (within-bundle linking) is undecided.长程组合欠账：集束神经下 4–5 跳 28.9%，低于早期未集束配置。恢复路径（束内连法）未裁定。
  4. No paraphrase-level answering: the read-only interface returns stored originals verbatim; multi-hop chaining over them is performed by the caller.无释义级答题：只读接口逐字返回存储原文；其上的多跳串联由调用方完成。
  5. Entity-level death is not population control: storage-level quad death runs autonomously; vitality-based entity death is wired but weakly binding against split-driven growth.实体级死亡非种群控制：存储层四元死亡自主运行；活力实体判死已接线，但对分裂驱动的增长约束力有限。
  6. Scale wall, stated: the field step builds dense n×n matrices each cycle. Graph cost is solved (bundling); field computation awaits a blocked/streaming implementation.规模墙，明示：场步每周期构建稠密 n×n 矩阵。图成本已由集束解决；场计算待分块/流式实现。
  7. Scope: the symbolic multi-hop task is not evidence of general intelligence. No official-benchmark superiority is claimed anywhere on this page.范围：符号多跳任务非通用智能证据。本页任何处不声称官方基准优越性。

7Positioning & citation定位与引用

Near-term, Valhalla is a persistent, auditable structural memory and adaptive agent-state substrate: state that can be frozen, transported, diffed and re-run bit-exactly. The longer-term research objective is native capability formation in the structure itself. The programme is funded against pre-registered technical milestones; each Class-B item above is a candidate either for promotion to Class A or for withdrawal.近期，Valhalla 是持久、可审计的结构记忆与自适应智能体状态基底：状态可冻结、运输、比对并逐字复跑。长期研究目标是结构本身的原生能力形成。本项目按预注册技术里程碑融资；上述每个 B 级项都是晋升 A 级或撤下的候选。
